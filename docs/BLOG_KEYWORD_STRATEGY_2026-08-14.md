# CampCareer Blog Keyword Strategy — 2026-08-14

## Objective

Use editorial SEO/GEO to acquire users on high-intent questions that naturally lead into a canonical Career Page.

The Blog does not compete with the Career Page for the same head term. Keyword ownership is deliberate.

## Keyword ownership

| Surface | Primary intent | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Career Page | Career verdict, demand, pay, entry, evidence | `electrician career australia`, `is electrician a good career in australia` |
| Blog | Informational sub-query with a direct Career transition | `electrician salary australia`, `how to become an electrician in australia` |
| Programs | Course/provider discovery after Career choice | `electrician course australia`, specific provider/program queries |
| Jobs | Current job-market action | `electrician jobs australia` |

This prevents the Blog from cannibalising the core Career URL.

## Evidence available now

- Care Worker

This gives CampCareer enough primary-source evidence to compete on answer quality rather than generic list content.

## P1 expansion

After P0 pages start collecting impressions:

- `[career] salary by state australia`
- `[career] apprenticeship australia`
- `[career] qualification australia`
- `[career] registration australia`
- `[career] career change australia`
- `[career] salary vs [adjacent career] australia`
- `[career] degree vs apprenticeship australia`

Only create a separate URL when the query has distinct intent and enough evidence to justify a complete answer. Otherwise expand the existing article.

## Content format for salary queries

A salary article should contain:

1. Direct answer in the opening paragraph.
2. Current official median weekly/hourly earnings where available.
3. CampCareer Score and the Demand / Pay / Entry dimensions.
4. Explanation of what can move pay up or down without inventing unsupported location numbers.
5. The qualification/licensing or registration path.
6. Clear distinction between median earnings, award minimums and advertised salaries.
7. CTA to the canonical Career Page.
8. Primary sources and last-reviewed date.

## Content format for how-to-become queries

1. Short path summary.
2. Required qualification or approved program.
3. Apprenticeship, placement or supervised-work requirement where applicable.
4. Licensing/registration authority.
5. Time/cost only where a reliable source is available.
6. Current Career Score and labour evidence as a reason to investigate the path, not as a guarantee.
7. CTA to the Career Page, then Programs.

## GEO answer design

Every article should make the answer easy for search and answer engines to extract without reducing the article to thin FAQ copy:

- state the answer before the background
- use factual subheadings matching real questions
- use source-backed tables
- separate facts from CampCareer interpretation
- show the review date
- cite primary authorities in the article
- avoid unsupported superlatives such as `best`, `guaranteed`, `easy PR`, or `high demand` unless the underlying evidence and definition are explicit

## Measurement gate

After deployment, collect query-level GSC data before expanding beyond the first cluster. Track:

- impressions by query
- average position
- CTR
- indexed/not-indexed state
- organic landing page
- click-through from Blog to Career Page
- Save/Compare actions after organic entry

The editorial roadmap should be re-ranked by actual CampCareer query data, not by third-party volume estimates alone.
