# CampCareer Brand System v1

## Purpose

CampCareer scores careers and shows people the path to get there. The brand system exists to make the CampCareer Score a recognizable product asset across Career Pages, search, sharing, TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts.

The visual system should feel credible, clear, decisive, modern and restrained. It must be simple enough to understand immediately and trustworthy enough to support consequential career decisions.

Internal direction: **Decision-grade Career Intelligence**.

Visual direction: **Score-led Editorial Utility**.

Core principle: **CampCareer Score is the hero, not decoration.**

## Brand character

Priority order:

1. Credible
2. Clear
3. Decisive
4. Modern
5. Ambitious
6. Approachable

CampCareer should communicate confident conclusions with transparent evidence. It should not look like a university directory, immigration portal, government data site, HR SaaS dashboard, generic AI startup or gamified career quiz.

## Typography

CampCareer uses Inter as the primary sans-serif typeface. The same family should carry career names, score numbers, verdicts, labels and body copy. Score numerals use tabular numerals where alignment matters.

Typography is information-led. The total score is the strongest numeric element. Decorative display type is not part of the core Score identity.

Uppercase is restricted. Product UI uses normal title and sentence casing. Social covers may use uppercase for Career and Country when additional attention is useful.

## Spacing and surfaces

Use a 4px-based spacing system with 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48 and 64 as the main working values.

Use restrained radii:

- small controls: 6px
- standard surfaces: 8px
- large surfaces: 12px

Avoid oversized rounded cards. Pill shapes are reserved for genuine tags and status labels. Shadows should represent real elevation only, not decorate the Score.

Semantic support colors:

- Verified / positive: `#18794E`
- Caution / limited evidence: `#8A6A16`

Color rule: **Blue brand, neutral judgment, restrained semantics.**

Demand, Pay and Entry do not receive separate category colors. Verdicts do not turn the primary Score Hero into a traffic-light system. Excellent, Strong, Mixed, Challenging and Tough are primarily communicated by words and numbers.

Approximate usage target:

- neutral colors: 80–90%
- brand blue: 5–10%
- semantic colors: 5% or less

## CampCareer Score visual identity

The default Score object is:

```text
Career
Country

CampCareer Score

78
Strong

Demand  9
Pay     8
Entry   6

High demand. Strong pay. Harder entry.
```

Rules:

1. The total score is the largest numeric element.
2. The verdict sits directly with the total score.
3. Demand, Pay and Entry always appear in that order.
4. The three dimensions do not use separate colors.
5. The default Score is not circular and does not use a gauge, speedometer, stars or progress ring.
6. `/100` and `/10` are supporting information, not dominant visual elements.
7. The Hero prefers numbers over charts.
8. Evidence confidence is visually separate from the score verdict.
9. Missing required evidence uses `Score not ready yet`; CampCareer does not manufacture a total.
10. The Score should remain recognizable even when the CampCareer logo is hidden.

## Career Page Hero

Information hierarchy:

1. Career
2. Country
3. CampCareer Score
4. Total score
5. Verdict
6. Demand / Pay / Entry
7. One-line interpretation
8. Main reason
9. Main blocker
10. Evidence confidence

The Hero gives the verdict before asking the user to do anything. Save, Compare, personalisation, visa details, programs and jobs remain secondary to the initial career judgment.

The Hero should not be dominated by occupational photography, large decorative gradients, methodology copy or multiple CTAs.

## Social template

TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts share one 9:16 master system.

Default sequence:

1. Career Hook: Career + Country
2. Score Reveal: Score + Verdict + Demand / Pay / Entry
3. Interpretation: short reason + main blocker
4. Path CTA: full breakdown / path

The Score Reveal is the signature recurring frame. Its geometry, typography, dimension order and CampCareer Blue remain consistent across videos.

Social may use the Social Ink background with white score typography and CampCareer Blue Bright as a restrained accent. Verdict-specific full-screen green, yellow or red treatments are not part of the system.

## Social to website continuity

Social and Career Page must use the same career, country, score, verdict, Demand / Pay / Entry values and, where practical, the same short interpretation.

Social links should deep-link directly to the relevant Career Page rather than an acquisition landing page, login gate or dashboard.

Principle: **Social promises the verdict. The Career Page proves it and shows what to do next.**

## Copy system

Official score name: `CampCareer Score`.

Official verdicts:

- Excellent
- Strong
- Mixed
- Challenging
- Tough

Interpretation follows the fixed dimension order:

`Demand. Pay. Entry.`

Example:

`High demand. Strong pay. Harder entry.`

Standard labels:

- Main reason
- Main blocker
- Why this score
- Evidence confidence
- Score not ready yet
- See your path

Voice principle: **Short verdicts. Plain evidence. Clear next steps.**

CampCareer states the conclusion first, explains the evidence plainly and avoids guaranteeing an individual's outcome.
