# KR → AU mining Shorts playbook

This is the manual production contract until a FacelessShort connection is available. Every asset directs viewers to the Korean route search with a UTM URL; no Short sends people to a partner, a consultation form, or a paid guide.

## Campaign URL

Base:

```text
https://www.campcareer.com/ko?utm_source=youtube&utm_medium=shorts&utm_campaign=kr-au-mining&utm_content={topic}
```

## Required 30–45 second format

1. **Question** (0–3s): repeat one route-result question in Korean.
2. **Official evidence** (3–15s): show the exact source name and its checked date. Do not narrate beyond what the source supports.
3. **Action** (15–35s): state the one thing the viewer should verify before spending or applying.
4. **CTA** (35–45s): `한국 → 호주 → 광업 취업 경로를 검색해 출처 링크를 확인하세요.`

Every caption set must include: source operator, source URL, checked date, the conditional-outcome disclaimer, and the exact UTM link. Never say that a visa, job, specified-work credit, or salary is guaranteed.

## Review every Monday

Use the route events, not views, to decide whether to continue a theme:

1. `route_search_started`
2. `route_result_viewed`
3. `route_external_link_clicked` by `visa`, `course`, `job`, `employer`, or `map`
4. `route_request_submitted`

Do not build payment or download infrastructure until the corridor crosses the product-core demand gate.
