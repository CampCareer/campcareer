# AU Electrician Visa Accessibility Checkpoint

Verified: 2026-08-14

Scope: Australia × Electrician (General) visa-accessibility component only.

## Representative route

Primary route: Skilled Independent visa (subclass 189).

Home Affairs lists Electrician (General) ANZSCO 341111 as eligible for subclass 189 and multiple additional skilled routes, including 190, 491, 482 Core Skills and 186 Direct Entry. The occupation is listed on MLTSSL and CSOL with Trades Recognition Australia as the assessing authority.

The 4 June 2026 SkillSelect invitation round invited Electrician (General) at a minimum score of 65, confirming current practical subclass 189 applicability for the occupation.

TRA states that Electrician (General) 341111 applicants from all countries require an OSAP assessment unless applying under the stated 482 or 485 exceptions.
