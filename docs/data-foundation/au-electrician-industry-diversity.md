# AU Electrician Industry Diversity Checkpoint

Verified: 2026-08-14

Scope: Australia × Electrician (General) industry-diversity component only.

## Official evidence

Jobs and Skills Australia publishes industry information for the broader ANZSCO `3411 Electricians` occupation series. The occupation profile identifies the leading industries as:

- Construction
- Mining
- Electricity, Gas, Water and Waste Services

The source is ABS Labour Force Survey, 2025 four-quarter average, customised report. JSA also states that industries comprising less than 5% of the occupation are suppressed.

The public occupation page and current downloadable occupation-profile presentation do not publish the employment share for each listed industry. The available industry pages rank `3411 Electricians` among their largest occupations, but also do not provide the reverse occupation-by-industry employment shares needed for a defensible Herfindahl-Hirschman Index (HHI).

## Frozen CampCareer rule

Industry Diversity normally requires:

- comparable broad-sector categories;
- occupation employment shares by sector;
- approximately 80% or better usable coverage;
- a calculable HHI and top-industry share.

No sector shares are invented or inferred from rank order. The fact that three industries are listed, and that omitted industries are individually below 5%, is insufficient to reconstruct the full distribution or calculate HHI.

Therefore:

- `hhi = null`
- `topIndustrySharePct = null`
- `coveragePct = null`
- `comparableBroadSectors = false`
- evidence status = `insufficient_industry_coverage`
- Industry Diversity score = `0 / 5`

This is a conservative data-coverage zero, not a factual claim that Electrician employment is maximally concentrated in one industry.
